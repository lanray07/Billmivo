# Billmivo privacy notice draft

Billmivo stores household bills, payments, member names and notes, sharing allocations, reminders, provider details, chosen document attachments and display preferences on your device. You can use the tracker without an account. The offline build stores data in app-private storage and disables Android automatic backup.

The app requests notification permission on devices that require it after you enable bill reminders. Notifications hide bill names by default. You can change preview settings and disable reminders at any time.

Voice controls request microphone permission when you start listening. Your device’s installed speech service processes the audio. Depending on that service and its settings, speech processing may use a network connection. Billmivo provides text and touch controls and does not retain a recording. Spoken forecasts are played only when requested.

From **Privacy and backup**, you can delete local household records after confirming, protect opening the app with the device’s screen lock, or create and restore a passphrase-encrypted backup. The device handles authentication. Billmivo does not collect the device PIN. A backup contains household records and attachment copies. It omits purchases and device preferences. You choose the destination through Android’s file picker, which may offer a location from a cloud provider you use. Billmivo cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

You confirm before exporting reports and choose where they are saved or with whom they are shared. Removing the app or clearing its data through device settings removes the records held in its app storage. Copies exported or shared outside Billmivo are managed in their destination.

In the Amazon edition, purchase and restore send the Amazon app-specific user ID and purchase receipt ID to Billmivo’s HTTPS receipt verifier. That verifier forwards those identifiers to Amazon’s Receipt Verification Service and returns whether the receipt entitles the account to Premium. Billmivo does not send household records, recordings, documents or payment-card information with these requests. The verifier does not retain the receipt or user ID. Billmivo caches a verified entitlement for up to three days using an account hash stored on the device.

Billmivo support is available at `banksmi@mail.com`. Before publishing, confirm the publisher’s legal name and privacy contact, review the notice against the exact binary being submitted, and supply the URL of the approved public notice in the Developer Console.
